about & privacy

What this is

It was in my likes first shows the exact date you liked each song, so you can prove you were early. Enter a SoundCloud username to see every public like with its date, or load your Spotify Liked Songs from an Exportify file. Add a friend to see which songs you both like and who got there first, then export the list as a PDF.

How it works

  • SoundCloud: when you enter a username, this site asks SoundCloud for that account's public profile and public likes and shows them to you. Only public information is read. Private likes can't be seen.
  • Spotify: you export your own Liked Songs with Exportify, a free, independent tool, and open the file here. The file is read inside your browser and is never uploaded to this site.
  • PDF export is created in your browser.

What we store

  • This site has no accounts and keeps no database of users, usernames, or likes.
  • To keep things fast, your browser temporarily remembers results and loaded files for the current tab (session storage), and remembers whether you last used SoundCloud or Spotify. You can clear this anytime by closing the tab or clearing site data.
  • Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes standard request information, such as IP address, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.
